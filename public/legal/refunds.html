Refunds & cancellation

Last updated: November 2026

This page summarises how billing works for MySafeOps subscriptions. The binding rules are in our Terms of service (especially §7 and §19).

1. How billing works

Paid plans are organisation subscriptions billed through Stripe. List prices on the website are shown exclusive of VAT (UK) or the equivalent local tax where stated for other markets. Tax may be calculated at checkout where Stripe Tax is enabled.

2. Cancellation

You can cancel from the in-app billing portal (Stripe Customer Portal) or by emailing support@mysafeops.com. Access continues until the end of the current paid period unless the account is suspended under the Terms.

3. Refunds

Fees are non-refundable except where required by law or where we agree otherwise in writing. That includes partial periods, unused seats/capacity, and early exit mid-term (Terms §7.4 and §19.2).

If you believe a charge was made in error, contact us promptly with the invoice or Stripe receipt reference.

4. Trials

Evaluation periods are disclosed on the pricing page and in-product. After a trial ends, subscribe to keep editing. Existing data remains viewable and exportable subject to the Terms.

5. Contact

Billing questions: support@mysafeops.com
