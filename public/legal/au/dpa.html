Data Processing Overview (Australia)

Last updated: July 2026

When your Australian organisation uses MySafeOps cloud features, you are generally the organisation responsible for worker and site data, and MySafeOps processes that data on your instructions to provide the Service.

Subprocessors (summary)

  • Vercel — application hosting (may process in US/EU)
  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, edge functions
  • Cloudflare — optional Workers/D1/R2 sync when enabled
  • Stripe — payments (AUD subscriptions)

Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

Personal information may be disclosed to overseas recipients in the UK, EU, and US. We take reasonable steps to ensure recipients handle information in accordance with the APPs, including contractual clauses in our standard DPA available on request at privacy@mysafeops.com.

Security

HTTPS/TLS, role-based access within organisations, hashed passwords via the auth provider, and optional tamper-evident audit logging on higher tiers. See Security & trust.

Contact

Data processing enquiries: privacy@mysafeops.com
