Cookie Policy (Australia)

Last updated: July 2026 · Version 2026-07-au

MySafeOps uses a minimal cookie set to operate the web application. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking for Australian visitors.

Essential cookies

  • Authentication session — keeps you signed in (Supabase auth when cloud is enabled)
  • Security / CAPTCHA — Cloudflare Turnstile or hCaptcha where configured on login
  • Stripe — payment session cookies when you manage billing

Local storage

Offline workspace data (RAMS, permits, registers) may be stored in your browser (localStorage / IndexedDB) when you use local-first features.

Your choices

Blocking essential cookies will prevent sign-in and billing flows from working. You can clear site data in your browser settings at any time — export organisation data first if needed.

See also Privacy policy (Australia).
