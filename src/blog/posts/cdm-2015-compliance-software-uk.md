The principal contractor's laptop has twelve open tabs: CPP version 4 on SharePoint, an induction spreadsheet from March, a hot work permit photo on WhatsApp, and an email from the client asking whether F10 was submitted. The site manager is on the phone about a MEWP permit. Nobody can answer, in under a minute, who was on site yesterday when the near-miss happened.

That is the everyday CDM problem on UK construction sites. **CDM 2015** sets clear duties for clients, principal designers and principal contractors. Regulation 13 lists what the PC must arrange: suitable site induction, welfare, coordination of contractors, and a Construction Phase Plan that is actually followed. Software does not replace those duties. It makes them visible when HSE, a client or your own H&S adviser asks for proof.

This guide is for principal contractors and H&S leads choosing **CDM 2015 compliance software**: what to expect, what to avoid, and how it links to [permits](/blog/permit-to-work-app-uk), [RAMS](/blog/how-to-write-a-rams-uk) and [site inductions](/blog/site-induction-software-uk).

## What CDM 2015 expects from principal contractors

Regulation 13 is the backbone. In practice, a PC on a notifiable project must:

- Prepare and maintain a **Construction Phase Plan** before work starts
- Ensure cooperation between contractors and coordinate their work
- Provide suitable **site induction** for everyone who needs it
- Manage welfare, fire, traffic and emergency arrangements
- Pass relevant information to contractors and check they have adequate RAMS
- Liaise with the principal designer on residual design risk
- Ensure **[F10 notification](/blog/f10-notification-uk)** happened if the project is notifiable

None of that says "buy software". HSE accepts paper systems that work. The gap appears at scale: multiple subbies, agency labour, shifting work areas, and inspections that ask for records from three weeks ago.

| CDM duty | Paper reality | Software advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPP maintained | PDF in cabin, version unclear | Version history, linked to live hazards |
| Inductions | Signed sheets, illegible names | Timestamped, searchable by person/date |
| Contractor RAMS | Email attachments | Linked to trade, location, permit |
| Cooperation | Meeting minutes | Shared visibility on permits and briefings |
| Audit | Ring binder hunt | Export pack in minutes |

Small contractors have different pressures; see [CDM 2015 for small contractors](/blog/cdm-2015-small-contractor-uk) for the sole-trader angle. This article focuses on PCs running busy sites.

## Paper CPP folders vs CDM compliance software

A **Construction Phase Plan** on paper can be compliant on day one and useless by week three. The roof sequence changed, a new subbie arrived, the excavation permit system moved to digital, but the CPP still mentions the old crane laydown area.

CDM compliance software should treat the CPP as a **living document**:

- Sections for management structure, site rules, welfare, fire, traffic, and key hazards
- Links to trade-specific RAMS and permit types in use
- Revision log when the programme or method changes
- Easy export for client audits and [HSE visits](/blog/hse-inspection-construction-uk)

What it should not do: duplicate every RAMS into the CPP, or become a 200-page PDF nobody reads on site.

## Core features to look for

**Construction Phase Plan builder**
Template aligned to HSE expectations, editable per project, with clear version control. Must match what is in our [CPP template guide](/blog/construction-phase-plan-template-uk).

**Site induction workflow**
Video, quiz or acknowledgement; records tied to person and date; works on mobile for agency staff. Induction before turnstile where possible.

**Contractor and RAMS library**
Upload or link subcontractor RAMS; tag by trade; show expiry or revision status. PCs should see which RAMS cover today's hot work, not search email.

**Permit to work integration**
[Hot work](/blog/hot-work-permit-uk), [height](/blog/height-work-permit-uk), [excavation](/blog/excavation-permit-uk) and other permits in the same system as CPP and inductions. CDM coordination fails when permits live in a separate app.

**F10 and project metadata**
Store F10 confirmation reference, client details, PD/PC appointments. Not every platform files F10 for you, but it should record that notification happened.

**Audit export**
One-click or scheduled export: inductions, permits, toolbox talks, CPP version. Essential before client H&S audits.

**Offline mobile**
Sites with poor signal still need inductions and permit sign-off. Cloud-only tools frustrate supervisors.

**Pricing model**
Per-seat pricing punishes CDM compliance because every operative needs access. Compare [construction safety app pricing](/blog/construction-safety-app-pricing-uk-2026) before you sign.

## How CDM software connects to permits and RAMS

CDM is the umbrella. **RAMS** describe how each contractor works. **Permits** authorise high-risk tasks on the day. Compliance software wins when these three layers talk to each other:

1. Subbie uploads RAMS → PC approves → linked to their induction
2. Supervisor issues height permit → RAMS reference auto-suggested
3. HSE asks about Tuesday's roof work → filter permits + inductions + toolbox talk for that date

Without links, you own three systems and a spreadsheet to glue them. That is how WhatsApp photos become your permit register.

## F10, PCI and designer cooperation

**Pre-construction information (PCI)** from the client should feed the CPP and designer's risk register. Software should store PCI centrally so subbies receive relevant sections at induction, not a generic pack. See [pre-construction information UK](/blog/pre-construction-information-uk) for what clients must provide.

**Principal designer cooperation** (Regulation 11) means sharing design risk information and updating it when the design changes. A PC platform that only tracks site activity misses half of CDM. At minimum, store PD contact, design change log, and residual risk notes accessible to supervisors. For Reg 12-14 PC duties in plain English, see [principal contractor duties UK](/blog/principal-contractor-duties-uk).

**F10** is separate from the CPP but auditors expect both. Record submission date and reference; see [F10 notification UK](/blog/f10-notification-uk).

## Implementation without killing site adoption

CDM software fails when only the H&S manager can use it. Practical rollout:

**Week 1:** CPP and project setup in office; F10 and PCI uploaded; induction template tested.
**Week 2:** Supervisors trained on permit issue and close-out on mobile.
**Week 3:** Subbie RAMS uploaded; first trades inducted on the app before site entry.
**Ongoing:** Toolbox talks recorded in the same system; CPP reviewed when programme shifts.

Keep paper backup for one month if crews are sceptical, but do not run duplicate systems forever. Pick one source of truth.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is CDM compliance software a legal requirement?**
No. CDM 2015 requires effective management arrangements. Software is one way to demonstrate them.

**What is the difference between a CPP and CDM compliance software?**
The CPP is the document. Compliance software is the system that creates, updates, distributes and evidences the CPP plus linked records.

**Do small principal contractors need CDM software?**
Not always. One site with a stable crew may manage on paper. Multiple subbies, agency labour or client audit requirements usually push firms digital.

**Can CDM software submit F10 to HSE?**
Most platforms record F10 confirmation rather than submitting directly. Submission stays on HSE's online system; store the reference in your project file.

**How does CDM software help during an HSE inspection?**
Faster retrieval of CPP, inductions, permits and cooperation records. Inspectors still walk the site; organised records reduce depth of document review.

**What should I ask in a demo?**
Show me Tuesday's induction list, open hot work permits, CPP version history, and export for a client audit. If any step takes more than two minutes, keep looking.
