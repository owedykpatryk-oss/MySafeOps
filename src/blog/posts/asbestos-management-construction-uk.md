The strip-out team starts on a 1970s office block. Nobody has seen an asbestos survey. By lunchtime someone has drilled through a textured ceiling tile. The client says the building was "cleared years ago" but cannot produce the report. Work stops. Costs spiral. HSE asks who had the duty to pass the information.

That is routine UK refurbishment. **Asbestos** is not a historical problem. The Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012 (CAR 2012) and CDM 2015 place clear duties on clients, designers and contractors. Construction teams need practical knowledge of surveys, work classifications, and records.

## Why asbestos still dominates UK refurbishment risk

Buildings before 2000 may contain asbestos in:

- Ceiling tiles, textured coatings (artex), insulation board
- Pipe lagging, gaskets, floor tiles, roofing felt
- Fire doors, risers, plant rooms

Disturbance without controls releases fibres. Unlike [silica dust](/blog/silica-dust-construction-uk), asbestos risk is often invisible until material is disturbed.

## Legal framework: CAR 2012 and CDM

| Duty | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **Identify asbestos in building** | Client / building owner |
| **Provide pre-construction information** | Client to designers and contractors |
| **Plan work to avoid disturbance** | Designer and contractor |
| **Manage notifiable/licensed work** | Licensed or competent contractors |
| **Prevent unauthorised access** | Principal contractor on site |

Under CDM, asbestos information belongs in **PCI** before work starts. Principal contractors should not mobilise strip-out without a suitable survey. See [principal contractor duties UK](/blog/principal-contractor-duties-uk).

## Survey types and when you need each

**Management survey**
Ongoing occupation, minor works, planning. Locates asbestos that could be disturbed during normal occupancy.

**Refurbishment and demolition survey**
Required before intrusive refurbishment or demolition. Fully intrusive, identifies materials in the scope of work.

**Reinspection**
Management surveys need periodic reinspection (typically annual) if asbestos remains in situ.

If the survey does not cover the area you are about to open up, stop and reassess. "We only chased a wall" is not an excuse when the wall contains AIB.

## Licensed vs notifiable vs non-licensed work

**Licensed asbestos work**
High-risk work (e.g. much pipe lagging, sprayed coatings). HSE-licensed contractor only.

**Notifiable non-licensed work (NNLW)**
Lower risk but still notifiable to HSE. Competent training, medical surveillance, notification rules.

**Non-licensed work**
Minor, short-duration tasks on materials in good condition with low fibre release, when risk assessment shows controls suffice.

Wrong classification is a common prosecution theme. When unsure, get a UKAS-accredited surveyor and competent asbestos analyst involved before the first break-out.

## What principal contractors must record

- Survey report and scope in project file
- Asbestos register shared with subbies doing strip-out
- RAMS for asbestos tasks linked to [how to write RAMS UK](/blog/how-to-write-a-rams-uk)
- Permits for enclosed work, negative pressure, waste routes if your [PTW system](/blog/permit-to-work-app-uk) uses them
- Waste consignment notes and clearance certificates
- Briefings: [toolbox talks](/blog/digital-toolbox-talks) before intrusive work
- Stop-work authority when unknown material appears

Digital project files help on [HSE inspections](/blog/hse-inspection-construction-uk) when you must prove information flow from client to operative.

## Common site failures

- **No refurbishment survey** before intrusive work
- **Survey in drawer**, trades not briefed
- **Subbie drills** through suspect board "just for one fixing"
- **Licensed work** done by general labour with masks
- **Waste mixed** with general skip
- **Clearance certificate** missing before re-occupation

Link asbestos controls to [construction site security](/blog/construction-site-security-uk): unauthorised trades in vacant buildings cause exposure.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is responsible for asbestos surveys on construction projects?**
The client must provide information about asbestos in the building. Surveys are commissioned by the client or building owner.

**What is the difference between management and refurbishment surveys?**
Management surveys support ongoing use. Refurbishment/demolition surveys are intrusive and required before work that disturbs the fabric.

**Can general construction workers remove asbestos?**
Only where risk assessment shows non-licensed work with correct training and controls. Licensed work needs HSE-licensed contractors.

**What is notifiable non-licensed asbestos work?**
Work that is not licensed but still meets HSE notification criteria. Competence, medical surveillance and notification rules apply.

**Should asbestos be in the Construction Phase Plan?**
Yes. Include how information is shared, stop-work rules, and waste routes.

**How does asbestos relate to pre-construction information?**
PCI must include available asbestos reports and registers. PCs should not start intrusive work without it.
