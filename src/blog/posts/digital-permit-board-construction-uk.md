The outgoing night supervisor wrote "HOT WORK BAY 3" on the whiteboard in red marker. By morning the job finished, the board was not wiped, and the day shift refused welding until someone found the paper permit in the welder's van. The board said live. The system, if there was one, said closed.

UK sites have used **permit boards** for decades: cabin whiteboard, clipboard grid, or laminated sheet taped to the hoarding. They answer one question: **what high-risk work is authorised right now?** The problem is they lie by lunchtime unless someone updates them every time a permit changes.

A **digital permit board** pulls status from the [permit to work app](/blog/permit-to-work-app-uk) so supervisors, H&S advisers and clients see the same live list.

## What a permit board is for on UK sites

Permit boards sit alongside RAMS. RAMS say how work should be done. Permits say what is authorised today for:

- [Hot work](/blog/hot-work-permit-uk)
- [Height](/blog/height-work-permit-uk)
- [Excavation](/blog/excavation-permit-uk)
- [Electrical isolation](/blog/electrical-isolation-permit-uk)
- [Lifting](/blog/lifting-operations-permit-uk)
- [Confined space](/blog/confined-space-permit-uk)

The board is the at-a-glance view for shift briefings, fire watch coordination, and interface meetings between trades.

## Whiteboard vs digital permit board

| | Cabin whiteboard | Digital permit board |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Update method** | Manual wipe and rewrite | Auto from permit actions |
| **Shift handover** | Depends on marker note | Timestamped history |
| **Search last Tuesday** | Impossible | Filter by date/area |
| **Offline plant rooms** | Paper duplicate often appears | Needs [offline PTW](/blog/ptw-software-offline-uk) |
| **Client audit** | Photo of board if lucky | Export PDF/CSV |
| **Cost** | Marker pen | Software subscription |

Whiteboards still work on tiny sites with one supervisor and three permit types. They fail on multi-trade jobs where permits open and close hourly.

## What a good digital board shows

Minimum columns UK supervisors expect:

- **Permit ID and type** (hot work, height, etc.)
- **Location / grid** or asset tag
- **Status** (live, suspended, closed, expired)
- **Authoriser and times** (issue, extension, close-out)
- **Trade / company** responsible
- **Linked RAMS reference**

Nice to have: map pin, photo thumbnail, fire watch name for hot work, wind speed note for height.

The board is read-only for most users. Issue and close happen on mobile; the cabin screen refreshes.

## Shift handover and multi-area sites

**Handover failures** the board should prevent:

- Closed hot work still listed live (fire watch confusion)
- Height permit expired after wind gust; board not updated
- Two excavation permits same grid line from different subbies
- Night shift isolation not shown to day shift electrical team

Digital boards timestamp every change. Incoming supervisors brief from the screen, not from whether the marker is red or black.

On **multi-gate sites**, one cloud board beats three cabin copies. Large civils projects sometimes mount a rugged tablet at each gate showing the same filtered view.

## Audit and client requirements

Principal contractors and clients increasingly ask for:

- Permits active on incident date
- Proof of fire watch close-out
- Cross-check with induction records on [HSE visits](/blog/hse-inspection-construction-uk)

A whiteboard photo does not survive that query. Digital boards backed by exportable records do.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a digital permit board?**
A live dashboard showing open and recently closed permits from your PTW software, replacing manual whiteboard updates.

**Do UK sites legally need a permit board?**
HSG250 expects permit systems with clear status. A visible board (physical or digital) is standard practice.

**Can we keep a whiteboard and digital system?**
Short transition only. Dual systems diverge. Pick one source of truth.

**Does the board work offline?**
The display may need cached data. Issue/close should work offline on phones; see [PTW offline UK](/blog/ptw-software-offline-uk).

**Who should see the permit board?**
Supervisors, H&S, client reps with access, fire marshals. Not a public internet page.

**How does this relate to RAMS?**
RAMS are documents. The board shows which permitted tasks are live now.
