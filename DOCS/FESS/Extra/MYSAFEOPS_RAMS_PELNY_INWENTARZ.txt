2. STRUKTURA DOKUMENTU RAMS (Risk Assessment & Method Statement)
================================================================================

Dokument RAMS w MySafeOps sklada sie z nastepujacych sekcji (druk/PDF):
  [cover] Cover & details — wymagana, domyslnie wlaczona
  [weather] Site weather — domyslnie wlaczona
  [map] Map / location link — domyslnie wlaczona
  [hospital] Nearest A&E / hospital — domyslnie wlaczona
  [operative_certs] Operative competencies — opcjonalna (opt-in)
  [hazards] Risk assessment matrix — wymagana, domyslnie wlaczona
  [signatures] Operative signatures — wymagana, domyslnie wlaczona
  [integrity] Document integrity — domyslnie wlaczona

Pola metadanych dokumentu RAMS:
  - title, location, leadEngineer, jobRef, siteId, projectId
  - scope (zakres prac / opis zadania)
  - methodSteps[] (kroki metody bezpiecznego wykonania — Method Statement)
  - hazards[] (macierz oceny ryzyka — Risk Assessment Matrix)
  - operativeSignatures[] (podpisy operatywow)
  - weatherSnapshot, hospitalInfo, mapLink, operativeCerts
  - printSections (ktore sekcje drukowac)
  - documentContentHash (integralnosc dokumentu)

Kazdy wiersz hazardu (hazards[]) zawiera:
  - category, activity, hazard
  - initialRisk: { L (1-5), S (1-5), RF (L×S) }
  - controlMeasures[] (srodki kontroli)
  - revisedRisk: { L, S, RF }
  - ppeRequired[] (wymagane SOI)
  - regs[] (przepisy UK: HASAWA, CDM, PUWER, LOLER, itd.)
  - permitTypes[], requiredCerts[] (opcjonalnie)

================================================================================
3. MODUL METHOD STATEMENT (osobny od RAMS)
================================================================================

Method Statement to osobny typ dokumentu (modul: method-statement).
Zawiera sekwencje krokow bezpiecznego wykonania prac bez pelnej macierzy ryzyka.
Mozna zapisac jako szablon (Document Templates) i powiazac z RAMS.

5. TRADE RAMS STARTERS (startery branzowe — scope + method + hazard tokens)
================================================================================

  STARTER: General construction (key: general)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      General construction and maintenance activities with CDM coordination, permit interfaces, and daily briefing before start.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Pre-start briefing and permit checks.
      2. Set exclusion zones and pedestrian/plant segregation.
      3. Verify tools, PPE and competence for the task.
      4. Execute work under supervisor control with stop-work authority.
      5. Close-out inspection and handover notes.
    Hazard tokens: manual handling, slips, trip, work at height, moving plant, pedestrian, noise, dust, adverse weather
    Kategorie: General, Groundworks, Access

  STARTER: Electrical & M&E (key: electrical)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Electrical isolation, verification and controlled maintenance with LOTO, test-before-touch and permit interfaces.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Confirm isolation plan and competent persons.
      2. Apply LOTO and verify dead before work.
      3. Test-before-touch and prove dead where required.
      4. Control hot work and fire watch interfaces.
      5. Re-energisation only under authorised procedure.
    Hazard tokens: electrical, live cable, switchgear, isolation, loto, hot work, arc, test-before-touch, manual handling
    Kategorie: Electrical, Hot works, General

  STARTER: Building & refurbishment (key: refurb_build)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Refurbishment and fit-out in occupied or live buildings — dust, noise, access, snagging and client interface controls.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Walk-round and client/site induction.
      2. Set dust/noise controls and protected routes.
      3. Sequence intrusive works with isolation checks.
      4. Log snags and defects during progress inspections.
      5. Handover with snag close-out evidence.
    Hazard tokens: refurb, fit-out, dust, noise, manual handling, work at height, slips, trip, occupied, hot work
    Kategorie: General, Access, Hot works

  STARTER: Groundworks & excavation (key: groundworks)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Excavation, groundworks and demolition interfaces with permit-to-dig, buried services and temporary works controls.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Verify utility records and scan proof before dig.
      2. Mark tolerance zones and establish banksman controls.
      3. Excavate in stages with edge protection.
      4. Record exposures and temporary works checks.
      5. Backfill/reinstate and close permit.
    Hazard tokens: excavation, dig, buried, utility, permit-to-dig, plant, collapse, manual handling, traffic
    Kategorie: Groundworks, General, Plant

  STARTER: Utilities (water, gas, electric, telecom) (key: utilities)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Utility installation, repair and mapping with permit-to-dig, isolation, pressure testing and streetworks controls.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Obtain utility records and agree isolation points.
      2. CAT/Genny scan and mark services before breaking ground.
      3. Execute works under permit with competent operatives.
      4. Pressure test / commission per network operator procedure.
      5. Reinstate and issue handover records.
    Hazard tokens: utility, water main, gas, sewer, jetting, cable, fibre, excavation, confined, traffic
    Kategorie: Utilities — Water & Sewer, Utilities — Gas & Electric, Groundworks & Excavation

  STARTER: Highways & streetworks (key: highways)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Road and footway works with Chapter 8 traffic management, NRSWA notices and live-traffic interface controls.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Agree TM plan and NRSWA/streetworks notices.
      2. Deploy signing, lighting and barriers before work starts.
      3. Maintain TM throughout; check after setup and hourly.
      4. Coordinate plant movements with banksman.
      5. Remove TM and reinstate to spec.
    Hazard tokens: traffic, chapter 8, nrswa, surfacing, live traffic, pedestrian, plant
    Kategorie: Highways & Streetworks, Chapter 8 Traffic Management

  STARTER: Rail & trackside (key: rail)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Trackside and station works with PTS competence, possession rules and OLE/third-rail awareness.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Confirm possession / isolation and site-specific rail induction.
      2. Brief team on OLE/third-rail and exclusion zones.
      3. Execute works under COSS/ES supervision as required.
      4. Maintain lookout and communication protocols.
      5. Hand back and close permits.
    Hazard tokens: rail, trackside, ole, pts, electrified, possession
    Kategorie: Rail & Trackside

  STARTER: Demolition & strip-out (key: demolition)
    SCOPE (zakres):
      Controlled demolition and strip-out with asbestos survey, isolation and sequential dismantling.
    METHOD (metoda — kroki):
      1. Review pre-demolition survey and asbestos register.

11. PERMITY (Permit to Work — PTW) — WSZYSTKIE TYPY
================================================================================

Modul Permits obsluguje permit-to-work powiazany z RAMS (linkedRamsId).
Kazdy permit ma: checklist, pola dodatkowe, status (draft/review/active/closed).

  TYP: Hot work permit (key: hot_work)
    Opis: Welding, grinding, cutting, brazing, soldering — any work producing heat or sparks
    Checklist (10 punktow):
      - Area cleared of all combustible materials within 10 metres
      - 2 × 9kg fire extinguishers and fire blanket in position
      - Hot works equipment inspected and in good condition
      - Fire alarm isolated in work area (with supervisor approval)
      - Sprinkler heads protected where applicable
      - Fire watch person nominated and briefed
      - All drains, ducts and openings sealed against spark entry
      - Work area wetted down where appropriate
      - Permit duration agreed — maximum 8 hours
      - Post-work inspection after minimum 1 hour agreed
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Equipment to be used (equipment, typ: text)
      - Fire watcher name (fireWatcher, typ: text)
      - Post-work inspection time (postInspectionTime, typ: datetime-local)

  TYP: Electrical isolation permit (key: electrical)
    Opis: Safe isolation of electrical circuits, LOTO, live electrical work
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - Isolation point identified and confirmed
      - Isolation carried out by authorised person
      - Lock-off device applied and padlock secured
      - Warning notice posted at isolation point
      - Voltage indicator tested on known live source
      - System proved dead with voltage indicator (GS38 compliant)
      - Voltage indicator re-tested on known live source after proving dead
      - All connected equipment confirmed de-energised
      - Permit held by the person performing the work
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Circuit / board reference (circuitRef, typ: text)
      - Lock-out padlock number (lockoutRef, typ: text)
      - Authorised person (isolation) (authorisedPerson, typ: text)

  TYP: Work at height permit (key: work_at_height)
    Opis: Scaffold, MEWP, ladders, rope access — any work above 2m
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - Access equipment inspected and signed off before use
      - Scaffold: handover certificate reviewed and current
      - MEWP: daily pre-use check completed; operator holds IPAF card
      - Edge protection confirmed in place at all open edges
      - Harness and lanyard inspected; attached to anchor point
      - Exclusion zone established below work area
      - Weather conditions assessed — not commenced in high winds or ice
      - Rescue plan in place for MEWP/rope access operations
      - Overhead hazards identified and communicated
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Access equipment type / ref (accessEquipment, typ: text)
      - Maximum working height (m) (maxHeight, typ: number)
      - Rescue plan reference (rescuePlan, typ: text)

  TYP: Confined space entry permit (key: confined_space)
    Opis: Entry into tanks, vessels, sewers, voids — any confined space
    Checklist (10 punktow):
      - Confined space risk assessment reviewed and current
      - Atmospheric test completed: O₂ (19.5–23.5%), CO (<20ppm), H₂S (<1ppm), LEL (<10%)
      - Continuous monitoring in use during occupation
      - Mechanical ventilation provided and confirmed operational
      - Stand-by person briefed and in position outside space
      - Rescue equipment (tripod, winch, harness) rigged and ready
      - Emergency rescue plan confirmed with stand-by person
      - All energy sources isolated (LOTO) before entry
      - Communication system tested between entrant and stand-by
      - Maximum occupancy and time in space agreed
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Space description / location (spaceDescription, typ: text)
      - Stand-by person name (standByPerson, typ: text)
      - Initial atmospheric readings (atmosphericReadings, typ: text)

  TYP: Permit to dig / excavation (key: excavation)
    Opis: Any excavation, ground disturbance, core drilling — utility strike prevention
    Checklist (10 punktow):
      - Utility drawings obtained from all relevant authorities
      - CAT scan (Cable Avoidance Tool) survey completed
      - Survey results marked out on ground before breaking
      - Hand dig zone (0.5m either side of marked service) confirmed
      - Excavation supervisor nominated and briefed
      - Shoring / trench support confirmed if depth exceeds 1.2m
      - Safe means of access and egress provided
      - Spoil stored minimum 0.5m from excavation edge
      - Barriers and covers in place over open excavation
      - Adjacent structures assessed for undermining risk
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - CAT scan carried out by (catScanBy, typ: text)
      - Known services in area (knownServices, typ: text)
      - Maximum excavation depth (m) (excavationDepth, typ: number)

  TYP: Lifting operations permit (key: lifting)
    Opis: Crane lifts, MEWP lifts, pallet truck lifts — all LOLER operations
    Checklist (10 punktow):
      - Lifting plan prepared by competent person
      - All lifting equipment within current LOLER thorough examination
      - Appointed Person (AP) present for crane lifts
      - Rigging plan reviewed — correct sling type, rating and angle
      - Load weight confirmed — does not exceed SWL of any component
      - Exclusion zone established below and around lift
      - Banksman / slinger in position with agreed signal system
      - Ground conditions checked — stable, level, adequate bearing capacity
      - Overhead hazards (power lines, structures) confirmed clear
      - Weather / wind speed assessed and within limits
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Lifting equipment / crane ID (liftingEquipment, typ: text)
      - SWL of equipment (tonnes) (swl, typ: number)
      - Appointed Person name (appointedPerson, typ: text)

  TYP: Cold work permit (key: cold_work)
    Opis: Maintenance on live plant without hot works — LOTO, mechanical isolation
    Checklist (8 punktow):
      - All energy sources identified (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravity, stored pressure)
      - Full LOTO applied to all energy isolation points
      - System depressurised and confirmed pressure-free
      - Stored energy (springs, counterweights) safely discharged
      - Permit holder has sole control of all padlocks
      - Try-out: attempt to start/operate equipment — confirmed no movement
      - Adjacent equipment not affected by isolation confirmed or separately isolated
      - Waste / drainage provisions in place for fluid release
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Equipment tag / ID (equipmentTag, typ: text)
      - Number of isolation points (isolationPoints, typ: number)
      - LOTO key holder name (lotoKeyHolder, typ: text)

  TYP: Line break permit (key: line_break)
    Opis: Opening any pressurised pipe, vessel or system containing fluid, gas or chemical
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - System isolated, depressurised and confirmed at atmospheric pressure
      - Contents identified — COSHH assessment reviewed for any chemical release
      - Drain / bleed point confirmed fully open before breaking joint
      - PPE specified for contents (chemical resistant, face shield, etc.) in use
      - Drip trays or containment in place for residual fluids
      - Adjacent systems confirmed isolated — no inadvertent pressurisation possible
      - Permit holder holds all isolation certificates
      - First break carried out slowly; standby for unexpected pressure release
      - Spill kit and emergency wash available at point of work
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Pipe contents (pipeContents, typ: text)
      - Normal working pressure (bar) (workingPressure, typ: number)
      - Contents temperature (°C) (temperature, typ: number)

  TYP: Roof access permit (key: roof_access)
    Opis: Any access to roof — flat, pitched, fragile or otherwise
    Checklist (10 punktow):
      - Pre-access roof condition survey completed
      - Fragile materials (rooflights, AC cement) identified and marked
      - Edge protection confirmed in place at all leading edges
      - Access route agreed — no walking on fragile surfaces
      - Crawling boards available for any fragile surface crossing
      - Weather assessed — not accessed in ice, snow or wind above 15 m/s
      - Maximum persons on roof agreed and not exceeded
      - Materials secured against wind displacement
      - No materials or tools left at roof edge unattended
      - Post-work roof inspection before closing permit
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Roof type (flat/pitched/fragile) (roofType, typ: text)
      - Access method (ladder/scaffold/hatch) (accessMethod, typ: text)
      - Maximum persons on roof (maxPersons, typ: number)

  TYP: Night / out-of-hours permit (key: night_works)
    Opis: Works outside normal operating hours — night, weekend, holiday
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - Site manager / client representative notified and approved out-of-hours working
      - Emergency contact for site owner confirmed and available
      - Security arrangements reviewed — site access and alarm procedures confirmed
      - Lone working risk assessment completed if applicable
      - First aider available or first aid arrangements confirmed adequate
      - All workers briefed on emergency procedures for out-of-hours
      - Adequate lighting confirmed before works commence
      - Noise restrictions for out-of-hours work identified and communicated
      - Check-in / check-out system in place
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Out-of-hours site contact / phone (siteContact, typ: text)
      - Security / alarm arrangements (securityCode, typ: text)
      - Lone working check-in interval (loneworkingArrangement, typ: text)

  TYP: Valve isolation permit (key: valve_isolation)
    Opis: Planned operation of process valves — food, pharma, chemical, water
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - P&ID reviewed — all valves to be operated identified
      - Upstream and downstream system status confirmed
      - Consequence of valve operation reviewed (product loss, pressure build, backflow)
      - All downstream operatives notified before valve operation
      - Valve position indicator / position confirmed before and after operation
      - Isolation confirmed by zero-flow check where applicable
      - Pressure differential across isolation confirmed safe
      - Blind flange / spade confirmation where positive isolation required
      - Valve closure documented in site log
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Valve tag number(s) (valveTag, typ: text)
      - System contents (systemContents, typ: text)
      - Authorised valve operator (authorisedOperator, typ: text)

  TYP: Visitor / contractor access permit (key: visitor_access)
    Opis: Authorised access for visitors, auditors, clients, new subcontractors
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - Visitor identity confirmed and signed in at reception
      - Site induction completed or confirmed current
      - PPE requirements communicated and confirmed compliance
      - Visitor escort assigned for duration of visit
      - Restricted areas identified — visitor not to enter without escort
      - Emergency procedure explained to visitor
      - Visitor contact details recorded
      - Purpose of visit recorded
      - Sign-out confirmed on departure
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Visitor company (visitorCompany, typ: text)
      - Purpose of visit (purposeOfVisit, typ: text)
      - Escort / host name (escortName, typ: text)

  TYP: Radiography permit (key: radiography)
    Opis: Gamma-ray or X-ray NDT inspection — ionising radiation source use
    Checklist (10 punktow):
      - Radiation Protection Supervisor (RPS) authorised the work in writing
      - Radiation Protection Adviser (RPA) consulted for non-routine operations
      - Controlled area barriers and warning signs in place (minimum 2m from source)
      - All persons in vicinity briefed — controlled area must remain clear
      - Dose rate survey completed; boundaries confirmed adequate
      - Dosimeters issued to all classified workers
      - Source transport container inspected; integrity confirmed
      - Emergency procedures confirmed with all personnel
      - Post-exposure survey completed; source confirmed returned to container
      - Dose records updated on completion
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Radiation source type (Ir-192, X-ray, etc.) (sourceType, typ: text)
      - Radiation Protection Supervisor name (rps, typ: text)
      - Controlled area radius (m) (controlledAreaRadius, typ: number)

  TYP: Ground disturbance permit (key: ground_disturbance)
    Opis: Piling, ground anchors, driven posts, any disturbance beyond CAT scan depth
    Checklist (9 punktow):
      - Ground investigation report reviewed — soil type, contamination, voids, buried structures
      - All utility records obtained; CAT scan completed
      - Archaeological or heritage desk-based assessment completed where required
      - UXO (unexploded ordnance) risk assessed — clearance certificate if applicable
      - Ground disturbance method approved by geotechnical engineer
      - Vibration monitoring on adjacent structures — alert thresholds set
      - Pre-work condition survey of all adjacent structures completed and photographed
      - Groundwater monitoring in place if dewatering required
      - Environmental protection: no ground disturbance adjacent to watercourse without EA consent
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Ground type / geology (groundType, typ: text)
      - Method of disturbance (disturbanceMethod, typ: text)
      - Maximum disturbance depth (m) (maxDepth, typ: number)

  TYP: Line clearance / product isolation (key: line_clearance)
    Opis: Food factory — mechanical work adjacent to open product lines; production isolated before entry
    Checklist (8 punktow):
      - Production manager sign-off — line stopped and cleared
      - Product isolation verified — no open product in work zone
      - Allergen changeover completed if crossing allergen zones
      - Tools and materials foreign-body checked (no glass, loose fixings)
      - G&HP register checked for items brought into zone
      - LOTO / isolation locks applied and verified
      - Hygiene barrier and boot-change procedure followed
      - Line restart briefing scheduled with production team
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Production line / zone (productionLine, typ: text)
      - Isolation / LOTO reference (isolationRef, typ: text)
      - Production manager sign-off name (productionSignOff, typ: text)

  TYP: Rail corridor access permit (key: rail_corridor_access)
    Opis: Survey or works within rail corridor — possession, PTS and safe system of work
    Checklist (8 punktow):
      - Possession / access window confirmed in writing with infrastructure manager
      - All personnel hold valid PTS (or client equivalent) and medical fitness
      - Lookout / COSS arrangements confirmed for non-possession works
      - Isolation of OLE / third rail confirmed where applicable
      - Emergency egress route and muster point briefed
      - Site-specific rail induction completed for all team members
      - Equipment and materials secured — no foul of running line
      - Handback inspection completed before line reopen
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Possession / access reference (possessionRef, typ: text)
      - PTS numbers (lead + team) (ptsNumbers, typ: text)
      - COSS / lookout name (cossName, typ: text)

  TYP: Marine / hydrographic works permit (key: marine_hydrographic)
    Opis: Bathymetric, hydrographic or vessel-based survey operations
    Checklist (8 punktow):
      - Marine risk assessment and MOB drill completed
      - Vessel/RHIB competency and insurance verified
      - Weather, tide and current limits agreed — stop-work criteria set
      - PFD/life jacket mandatory for all deck operations
      - Harbour master / port authority notification where required
      - Emergency shore contact and rescue plan in place
      - Survey equipment secured against overboard loss
      - Fuel and chemical handling per COSHH on vessel
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Vessel / craft name (vesselName, typ: text)
      - Coxswain / master name (coxswain, typ: text)
      - Approved tide / weather window (tideWindow, typ: text)

  TYP: Aerial survey coordination permit (key: aerial_survey_coordination)
    Opis: Drone, helicopter or fixed-wing survey — airspace, NOTAM and ground exclusion
    Checklist (8 punktow):
      - Flight plan and risk assessment approved by survey lead
      - CAA operator authorisation / A2 CofC or GVC verified for UAV
      - NOTAM or airspace coordination completed for manned aircraft
      - Landowner and client notifications for low-level overflight
      - Ground exclusion zone established under flight path
      - Weather limits (wind, visibility, precipitation) agreed
      - Emergency landing area identified for UAV operations
      - Post-flight equipment check and secure storage completed
    Pola dodatkowe:
      - Aircraft / UAV type (aircraftType, typ: text)
      - CAA operator ID / pilot licence (operatorId, typ: text)
      - NOTAM / airspace clearance ref (notamRef, typ: text)

  TYP: General permit to work (key: general)
    Opis: General works not covered by a specific permit type
    Checklist (6 punktow):
      - Work scope clearly defined and communicated to all operatives
      - Hazards identified and controls in place
      - Appropriate PPE issued and worn
      - Emergency procedures communicated
      - First aid arrangements confirmed
      - Permit area identified and access controlled
