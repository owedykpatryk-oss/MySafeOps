# Recoverable notes and cancellable exports

The user requested further repairs, improvements and deployment. Inspection found that the evidence form was keyed by mission revision: weather or other edits remounted it and discarded typing. Both evidence and field-session notes also lost unsubmitted text when their tab unmounted.

- Added immediate session-storage recovery scoped by organisation, country workspace, mission, user and form purpose. Drafts remain unsubmitted; no evidence or session event is created until the user saves. Storage failures preserve in-memory values and show a recovery warning.
- Evidence no longer remounts on every revision. A draft from an earlier planning revision retains its text and requires explicit review against the current plan before submission. Successful saves and explicit discard clear the draft. New typing starts against the current revision even if an untouched form was opened earlier.
- Field notes survive tab changes and reload. Reason suggestions append rather than overwrite existing text; choosing another action cannot silently replace a non-empty note. Stable accessible names let assistive technology identify the textareas after their content changes.
- Export sessions check whether the workspace is still active before requesting imagery, after it returns, between documents and immediately before download. The user can cancel an active export. A pending image request may finish or time out before cancellation completes, but the cancelled result cannot trigger a download. Leaving the module or changing organisation/country also invalidates that export.
