## How UK safety apps are priced

Most platforms fit one of three models:

| Model | How it works | Typical buyers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Per-seat (per user)** | Every named user with login pays monthly | Firms that want granular control |
| **Per-site** | Fixed fee per active project or site | Principal contractors with rotating sites |
| **Flat organisation** | One monthly fee covers all workers/projects | SMEs tired of counting seats |

Some vendors mix models: base platform fee plus per-seat above a threshold, or free workers but paid supervisors only. That hybrid can work: or it can mean operatives cannot sign permits without upgrading.

**What counts as a seat** varies. Ask explicitly:

- Do agency operatives need paid licences?
- Do client representatives pay for read-only access?
- Are archived projects still billed?
- Does offline mobile access cost extra?

## Per-seat vs flat org vs per-site

**Per-seat** scales with headcount. Example maths for a mid-size contractor:

- 25 supervisors/managers at £15/user = £375/month
- 60 operatives at £15/user = £900/month
- **Total £1,275/month** before VAT: and headcount rises in summer

**Per-site** suits firms running discrete projects with clear boundaries. A £800/month site licence looks expensive on a £200k refurbishment, cheap on a £40m frame package with 80 people. Watch renewal clauses when sites run 18 months.

**Flat organisation** charges one fee regardless of worker count. Example: £149–£399/month for unlimited worker accounts on some UK-focused platforms. Advantage: agency spikes and summer peaks do not trigger licence panic. Trade-off: feature tiers may gate permits, RAMS or integrations on lower plans.

**Per-site** quotes commonly **£500–£2,000 per site per month** at tier one; volume discounts apply for national contractors.

## Hidden costs buyers miss

- **Implementation and training**: £2k–£20k for enterprise; some SaaS includes remote onboarding, some does not.
- **Data migration**: old PDF RAMS, Excel registers, legacy permit history.
- **SMS or MFA charges**: rare but appear on some quotes.
- **Storage and photo limits**: inspection apps with heavy photo use can hit caps.
- **Client/subcontractor portals**: charged as extra seats.
- **Annual uplift**: 3–8% renewal increases in multi-year deals.
- **Minimum seat bundles**: "25 seat minimum" transforms a small trial into a £4k commitment.
- **Paid integrations**: Procore, Viewpoint, Power BI connectors on top tiers only.

When evaluating [paper vs digital RAMS](https://mysafeops.com/blog/paper-vs-digital-rams-uk), include admin time saved: but also true software TCO over 24 months, not just licence line items.

## What to budget by company size

**Sole trader or micro (1–5 people)**
- Budget **£0–£50/month** if digital at all.
- Prioritise: simple RAMS, induction evidence, photo inspections.
- Avoid per-seat models unless every operative needs a login.

**Small contractor (6–25 on site)**
- Budget **£50–£250/month** flat, or per-seat only if peak users under 15.
- Prioritise: [digital toolbox talks](https://mysafeops.com/blog/digital-toolbox-talks), inductions, COSHH register, basic permits.
- Compare at least two models using peak agency week headcount.

**Mid-size subcontractor (26–80 on site)**
- Budget **£250–£1,200/month** depending on permit complexity.
- Prioritise: full PTW lifecycle, offline mobile, subcontractor visibility, audit exports.
- Run a formal comparison: our [PTW software comparison](https://mysafeops.com/blog/best-permit-to-work-software-uk-2026) template helps.

**Principal contractor**
- Budget **enterprise sales process**: rarely transparent online.
- Prioritise: multi-site dashboard, F10/project file alignment, RIDDOR-ready records, API to ERP.
- Negotiate seat definitions before legal review.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. **What is the cost at peak headcount in July?** Model 90th percentile, not average.
2. **Do operatives need paid seats to sign permits or inductions?**
3. **Are client and auditor logins included?**
4. **What happens to price when we add a second site?**
5. **Is offline included or an add-on?**
6. **How are leavers and agency turnover handled?** (Seat recycling rules.)
7. **What is the renewal uplift cap?**
8. **Can we export our data if we leave?** (Format and fee.)
9. **Which features are plan-gated?** (PTW, RAMS builder, API.)
10. **Is UK support in timezone and regulation terms included?**

Get answers in writing. Sales calls promise "flexible"; contracts define reality.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does construction safety software cost in the UK?**
Typical range from free–£600/year for micro firms to £15,000–£60,000+/year for principal contractors. Model depends more than headline rate.

**Is per-user pricing cheaper for small teams?**
Sometimes for under ten users. Above twenty site staff, flat org pricing is often cheaper and easier to adopt.

**Are there free safety apps for UK construction workers?**
Some platforms offer free worker tiers or free trials. Check whether workers can complete inductions and permits or only view content.

**What is the hidden cost of paper RAMS?**
Admin time, reprint churn, audit prep and lost permits. Digital TCO comparisons belong in the same spreadsheet as licence fees.

**Should I pay per site or per organisation?**
Per-site if few long projects; per-organisation if you run many small jobs or rotating gangs across the year.
