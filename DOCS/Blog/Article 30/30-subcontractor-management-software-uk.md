Monday 06:30. Six firms on site. The M&E ganger says his RAMS were "sent last week". The cladding subbie has new agency lads not on the induction sheet. The principal contractor's H&S adviser opens an email chain 40 messages deep looking for insurance. Nobody knows which version of the crane lift RAMS is live.

That is **subcontractor management** in the wild. CDM calls it cooperation and coordination. Commercial teams call it admin pain. Software should answer three questions fast: **Who is allowed on site? What are they allowed to do? Can you prove it?**

## What subcontractor management means on UK sites

Subcontractor management is not procurement software. On site it means:

| Activity | PC responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Pre-start** | RAMS, insurance, competence evidence |
| **Mobilisation** | Induction, site rules, CPP sections |
| **Live work** | Permits, toolbox talks, coordination |
| **Audit** | Retrieve records by firm, date, location |

Each subcontractor still owns their method and supervision. The PC owns **coordination** across trades. See [principal contractor duties UK](https://mysafeops.com/blog/principal-contractor-duties-uk).

## CDM duties driving the software need

Regulation 13 requires PCs to:

- Give subcontractors relevant [CPP](https://mysafeops.com/blog/construction-phase-plan-template-uk) and [PCI](https://mysafeops.com/blog/pre-construction-information-uk) information
- Ensure subcontractors plan and manage their work
- Check RAMS are adequate before work starts
- Liaise with principal designer on residual risks

Software does not replace conversations. It stops RAMS approvals living in one person's inbox when that person is on leave during an [HSE inspection](https://mysafeops.com/blog/hse-inspection-construction-uk).

## Core features principal contractors need

**Subcontractor register**
Company details, contacts, scope, insurance expiry, SSIP or equivalent.

**RAMS library per firm**
Version control, approval status, link to tasks and [permits](https://mysafeops.com/blog/hot-work-permit-uk).

**Induction and workforce records**
Who from each firm is inducted, when, with acknowledgements. [Visitor management](https://mysafeops.com/blog/construction-site-visitor-management-uk) separate from operatives.

## Onboarding a new subcontractor

Standard PC workflow:

1. **Contract and scope** agreed commercially
2. **Request RAMS, insurance, training policy** via portal
3. **Review RAMS** against site risks and PCI
4. **Approve** with named reviewer and date
5. **Induct supervisors** before workforce arrives
6. **Issue permits** only against approved RAMS
7. **Toolbox brief** when conditions change ([summer heat](https://mysafeops.com/blog/summer-heat-safety-construction-uk), etc.)

Block turnstile or gate until steps 3-5 complete for high-risk trades.

## Common PC failures with subbies

- **RAMS approved without reading** (template recycling)
- **Insurance expired** mid-project
- **Agency swap** not re-inducted
- **Parallel permit systems** (subbie on paper, PC on app)
- **No interface meetings** when trades overlap
- **Subbie data siloed** per supervisor, not PC-wide

Fix with one system of record and gate rules PCs enforce, not suggest.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is subcontractor management software in construction?**
Platforms that track subcontractor firms, RAMS approvals, insurance, inductions, permits and audit records for principal contractors.

**Is it required under CDM 2015?**
Software is not required. Coordination and cooperation duties are. Software helps evidence them.

**Should subcontractors use the same app as the principal contractor?**
Often yes for inductions and permits. RAMS may be uploaded by subbie and approved by PC in the same system.

**How is this different from RAMS-only software?**
RAMS tools author documents. Subcontractor management adds firm-level compliance, workforce induction and cross-trade permit visibility.

**What about small sites with two subbies?**
Paper can work. Software pays back when firm count, agency labour or client audits increase.

**Can visitors and delivery drivers use the same system?**
Use visitor sign-in for short visits; full induction path for anyone entering work areas. See [visitor management UK](https://mysafeops.com/blog/construction-site-visitor-management-uk).
