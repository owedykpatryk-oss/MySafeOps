The fire alarm goes off at 11:40. The site manager grabs the clipboard by the turnstile. Three names are illegible, two delivery drivers left without signing out, and a client architect is somewhere on level four without a escort badge. The roll call takes twelve minutes. That is why **visitor management** on UK construction sites is a safety control, not reception polish.

CDM 2015 expects sites to prevent unauthorised access and to manage emergencies. Your visitor log is how you prove who was on site if HSE, the client or the fire service asks afterwards. It also sits in **GDPR** territory the moment you store names, companies, vehicle registrations or photos.

This guide covers practical UK visitor sign-in, data rules, and how digital logs connect to [site induction](https://mysafeops.com/blog/site-induction-software-uk) and [CDM compliance](https://mysafeops.com/blog/cdm-2015-compliance-software-uk).

## Why visitor management matters on UK sites

| Reason | What goes wrong without a log |
| --- | --- |
| **Fire evacuation** | Missing persons, delayed all-clear |
| **Security** | Theft, trespass, unauthorised photography |
| **Incident investigation** | Unknown witnesses or victims on site |
| **Client audit** | Cannot prove who attended site meetings |
| **Insurance** | Disputes over who was present when damage occurred |

Principal contractors carry coordination duty under CDM. See [principal contractor duties UK](https://mysafeops.com/blog/principal-contractor-duties-uk). Visitor control is part of site rules in the [Construction Phase Plan](https://mysafeops.com/blog/construction-phase-plan-template-uk).

## Types of visitor: delivery, client, inspector, public

**Delivery drivers**
Short visit, often unescorted to laydown. Still sign in and out. Brief on traffic routes and PPE at gate.

**Client and design team**
May need fuller [induction](https://mysafeops.com/blog/site-induction-software-uk) if entering active work areas. Escort rules should be in CPP.

**HSE and third-party inspectors**
Often unannounced. Pre-agree how they sign in and who escorts. During [HSE inspection season](https://mysafeops.com/blog/hse-inspection-construction-uk), a clear process reduces friction.

**Contractor visitors** (subbie supervisors, tool hire reps)
Not your employees. Treat as visitors unless on your induction register as workers.

**Members of public**
Should not access active sites. Fencing and signage are first controls; logging catches breaches.

## GDPR and data retention

UK GDPR applies to visitor logs as **personal data**.

Practical compliance:

- **Privacy notice** at sign-in (why you collect data, retention period)
- **Minimum data**: name, company, purpose, time in/out; photo only if justified
- **Retention limit**: often 3-12 months unless incident requires longer hold
- **Secure storage**: locked cabin or encrypted app; not a photo on WhatsApp
- **Subject access**: be able to export or delete on request

Vehicle registrations and CCTV stills are personal data. Treat the visitor log like HR records, not a scrap paper.

## Linking visitors to induction and permits

If a visitor enters an active work face, they need appropriate briefing, PPE and often an escort. Do not let "visitor" bypass [hot work](https://mysafeops.com/blog/hot-work-permit-uk) or [height](https://mysafeops.com/blog/height-work-permit-uk) controls.

Rules that work:

- **Green route**: site cabin and meeting room only, visitor badge, escort optional
- **Amber route**: active site with escort, short induction video
- **Red route**: no visitors without full induction and permit awareness

Link visitor policy to summer welfare: [heat safety](https://mysafeops.com/blog/summer-heat-safety-construction-uk) briefings apply to escorted clients too.

## Common failures at gate and cabin

- **Sign-in with no sign-out** (inflated headcount in emergencies)
- **One log for three gates** (people on site not recorded)
- **Visitor on crane deck without escort**
- **No privacy notice** on iPad sign-in app
- **Log stored in van** that leaves site daily
- **Delivery drivers bypassing gate** when programme pressure peaks

Fix with a single owner (gate supervisor), daily log review, and random audits matching turnstile CCTV to names.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a visitor log legally required on UK construction sites?**
CDM requires preventing unauthorised access and managing emergencies. A visitor log is standard evidence you know who is on site.

**Does GDPR apply to construction site visitor books?**
Yes. Names, companies, vehicle details and photos are personal data. Provide a privacy notice and retention policy.

**How long should visitor records be kept?**
Often 3-12 months unless needed for an incident investigation or contract. Document your policy.

**Should delivery drivers sign in?**
Yes. Short visits still matter for evacuation and security. Brief on traffic rules.

**Can visitor management integrate with site induction software?**
Yes on integrated platforms. Visitors needing work-area access may convert to a limited induction record.

**What is the difference between a visitor and a subcontractor operative?**
Operatives work under their employer's RAMS and need full induction. Visitors are temporary and usually escorted unless formally inducted.
