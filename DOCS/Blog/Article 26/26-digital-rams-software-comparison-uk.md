## HBXL Health and Safety Xpert

**Profile:** Long-established UK desktop toolkit for CDM documentation, risk assessments and method statements.

**Strengths**
- Familiar to UK H&S consultants and small builders
- CDM 2015 document wizards including CPP support
- One-off licence option suits micro firms avoiding subscriptions

**Limitations**
- Desktop-centric; field sign-off often still paper
- Multi-site visibility weaker than cloud platforms
- Permits and live site coordination usually separate systems

**Typical buyer:** Sole trader or small contractor writing their own RAMS in the office. Pair with [how to write a RAMS UK](https://mysafeops.com/blog/how-to-write-a-rams-uk) if templates are your main need.

## EcoOnline (formerly Alcumus)

**Profile:** Enterprise EHS platform with RAMS, incidents, audits and chemical management.

**Strengths**
- Broad EHS for firms with dedicated safety teams
- Reporting and analytics across multiple sites
- Chemical and COSHH modules overlap [COSHH register](https://mysafeops.com/blog/coshh-register-software-uk) needs

**Limitations**
- Implementation heavier than SME tools
- Per-user cost rises with agency labour
- Site supervisors may find full suite overkill on a single project

**Typical buyer:** Regional contractor or principal contractor with central H&S function.

## SmartQHSE

**Profile:** UK-visible platform mixing inspections, RAMS and compliance workflows.

**Strengths**
- Mobile inspections with photo evidence
- RAMS and checklist culture fits principal contractor audits
- Competitive for mid-market UK firms

**Limitations**
- Feature depth varies by subscription tier
- Permit-to-work may not match specialist PTW lifecycle needs
- Compare per-user cost at summer peak headcount

**Typical buyer:** Subcontractor or mid-size firm already using SmartQHSE for inspections wanting RAMS in the same app.

## HandsHQ and Nureaka

**Profile:** RAMS authoring platforms popular with UK subcontractors who respond to principal contractor template demands.

**Strengths**
- Fast RAMS creation from trade libraries
- Professional PDF output clients recognise
- Good for firms whose main pain is writing, not whole-site CDM

**Limitations**
- Often RAMS-centric; CPP, F10 and permit boards may live elsewhere
- Per-user pricing on large crews adds up
- Integration with client-mandated systems varies

**Typical buyer:** Specialist subbie (demolition, cladding, M&E) producing frequent job-specific RAMS.

## How to choose for your firm

**Micro / sole trader (1-5)**
HBXL or template-led RAMS authoring may suffice. Prioritise low setup and CDM document help.

**Small subcontractor (6-25 on site)**
Need mobile sign-off and fast revision. Compare HandsHQ, SmartQHSE entry tiers, MySafeOps on total cost.

**Mid-size with client audits**
Version history, search, export. SmartQHSE, EcoOnline SME tiers, or MySafeOps if permits matter daily.

**Principal contractor**
RAMS plus subcontractor coordination, CPP, inductions. [CDM compliance software](https://mysafeops.com/blog/cdm-2015-compliance-software-uk) lens beats RAMS-only tools.

**Questions for any demo**
1. Show operative sign-off on a phone without a paid seat.
2. Show RAMS version on a date three weeks ago.
3. Show export for a client audit in under five minutes.
4. Quote price at peak July headcount, not January average.
5. Does it link to permits or is RAMS a silo?

Also compare PTW-focused tools in [best permit to work software UK 2026](https://mysafeops.com/blog/best-permit-to-work-software-uk-2026) if permits drive your buying decision more than RAMS authoring.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best RAMS software for UK construction?**
Depends on firm size. Desktop tools suit micro firms; mobile platforms with sign-off suit active sites; enterprise suites suit multi-site H&S teams.

**Is HBXL still relevant in 2026?**
Yes for small UK firms wanting CDM document generation on desktop. Less suited when you need live mobile sign-off across large crews.

**How much does RAMS software cost in the UK?**
From roughly £200-£500/year for desktop licences to £3,000-£15,000+/year for per-user cloud platforms at mid-size headcount. See [pricing guide](https://mysafeops.com/blog/construction-safety-app-pricing-uk-2026).

**Do I need separate RAMS and permit software?**
Not always. Firms with heavy hot work, height and excavation often prefer integrated RAMS and PTW. RAMS-only tools work if permits are rare and paper PTW is acceptable.

**Can RAMS software replace a Construction Phase Plan?**
No. RAMS are trade-specific. The CPP is the principal contractor's site-wide plan. Good platforms link them.

**How do RAMS tools compare to SafetyCulture/iAuditor?**
iAuditor excels at inspections and checklists. UK RAMS depth varies. See [SafetyCulture alternative UK](https://mysafeops.com/blog/safetyculture-alternative-uk) for broader comparison.
