## The gap worth knowing about

The blog is **almost entirely safety-side**. Of 40 published guides, roughly 38 target permits, RAMS, CDM, RIDDOR and registers. There is essentially **no survey or PAS 128 content** — no `pas-128-survey-uk`, no `gpr-survey-report`, no `utility-survey-software-uk`.

That is the product's strongest differentiator with no search presence behind it.

**Recommendation:** the survey-side keywords below are an open goal. Social posts on PAS 128 and GPR should each have a matching blog guide so the social traffic has somewhere to land.

## Where to use them

**LinkedIn post body** — naturally, in the language you'd actually speak. The PAS 128 post uses M1/M2/M2P because that's what surveyors call them. Never force a keyword into a sentence; LinkedIn does not rank on density and it reads badly to humans.

**Instagram hashtags** — this is where niche terms genuinely find people. `#PAS128`, `#groundpenetratingradar`, `#COSS`, `#BRCGS` have small but exactly-right audiences. `#construction` has millions of posts and you will never surface in it.

**Profile bios** — both platforms. The bio is indexed and it is the one place a plain keyword string is acceptable. Suggested: *"Utility survey reporting and permit to work, in one place. PAS 128, GPR, RAMS. Built in the UK."*

**Blog** — each social topic should have a matching guide. The social post creates demand; the guide captures the search.

## Competitive positioning language

The blog already targets `safetyculture-alternative-uk`, so the competitive frame exists. Two honest positions for social:

**Against generic safety apps** — "they store your permits, they don't read them." The conflict matrix is the proof. Don't name competitors in social posts; the comparison is stronger implied.

**Against survey software** — "your CAD package doesn't know what a permit is." The survey-to-dig handover is the proof.

**The line that captures both:** *the only place the survey and the permit are the same record.*

Don't claim to be the best at either half. Claim the join, because that is the only claim that is both true and uncontested.
