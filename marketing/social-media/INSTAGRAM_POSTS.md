# Instagram Posts

10 posts, written Instagram-first — shorter, more direct, built around what the viewer can understand in three seconds.

**Reality check before you start:** Instagram is the weaker channel for this buyer. Survey company owners and contracts managers live on LinkedIn. Treat Instagram as reach, brand surface and recruitment — the mechanism here is that a field surveyor sees it, sends it to their boss, and the boss becomes the lead. Write for the person holding the tablet, sell to the person holding the card.

**Existing asset:** there is already a photo bank on Google Drive at `MySafeOps – Instagram` with documentary-style site photography. Several posts below reuse those images rather than needing new ones — noted where it applies.

# POST 8 — What GPR can't see

**Format: Carousel** · **Persona:** survey owner, client-side

### Opening line
> GPR does not see everything. That's physics, not a bad survey.

### Caption

GPR does not see everything. That's physics, not a bad survey.

Plastic pipe with no tracer wire is frequently invisible to it — plastic sections and repairs interrupt the signal.

And GPR will sometimes show you the trench rather than each service inside it. Useful information. Not the same as locating every asset.

None of this is a failing. But if the first time a client hears it is in the meeting about why something got struck, it sounds like an excuse.

Put it in the quote, not the apology.

# POST 9 — The bug that ate photos

**Format: Static, text-led** · **Persona:** all · **Build in public**

### Opening line
> A bug in my code deleted photos surveyors had already taken.

### Caption

A bug in my code deleted photos surveyors had already taken.

Sync loop. Poor signal, half-finished upload, screen open. The list rebuilt itself and treated a photo that existed locally but hadn't uploaded as deleted.

You can't re-run field data. The chamber's backfilled. The surveyor's forty miles away.

What changed: nothing hard-deletes any more. Everything goes to a recycle bin. Sync uses tombstones instead of removal.

The rule I should have started with — deleting should need more certainty than keeping. Absence usually means not yet, not gone.

## Instagram notes

**Cadence:** 3 per week. Tuesday, Thursday, Friday. Morning or lunchtime.

**Format mix:** roughly half Reels, a third carousels, the rest static. Reels carry reach; carousels carry saves; static carries the story posts.

**Hashtags:** 6–8 per post, mixing niche industry tags (`#PAS128`, `#COSS`, `#groundpenetratingradar`) with broader ones (`#construction`, `#healthandsafety`). The niche ones are where this account will actually get found — nobody is winning `#construction`.

**Captions:** first line is everything. Instagram truncates fast, so the hook has to survive on its own.

**Reuse the Drive bank.** Posts 4, 7 and 8 above already have photography shot. Don't re-generate what exists.

**Do not** copy the LinkedIn posts across. They are three times too long and the register is wrong.
