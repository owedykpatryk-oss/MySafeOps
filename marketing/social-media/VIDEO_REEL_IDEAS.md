# Video & Reel Ideas

12 concepts. Most are screen recordings — this product's best moments happen on a screen, and screen capture costs nothing to reshoot.

**Two rules for everything below:**
1. **Assume no sound.** Captions carry the whole message. Never rely on voiceover.
2. **Show the software doing something, not someone talking about it.** The moment the DXF opens, the moment the permit is refused — that's the content.

## Tier 1 — Make these first

### R1 — Photo in, CAD out
**Length 20s · Screen recording + site footage · Persona: surveyor**

The single most demonstrable thing this product does.

| Time | Shot | Overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Phone raised over open chamber, shutter | `Take the photo.` |
| 2–5s | Laptop, someone typing coordinates by hand | `Stop typing coordinates.` |
| 5–12s | Screen: GPS populates, National Grid appears, bearing arrow rotates | `GPS. National Grid. Bearing.` |
| 12–18s | DXF open in CAD, point in position | `Same photo. Now it's CAD.` |
| 18–20s | Tablet on chamber lid | `MySafeOps.` |

**Notes:** hold the cut at 12s a full beat longer than comfortable — that transition is the payoff. No music over the screen section.
**Honesty requirement:** the caption must carry the ±5 m note. Don't bury it.

### R2 — Watch it say no
**Length 18s · Screen recording · Persona: H&S manager, supervisor**

| Time | Shot | Overlay |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Finger on "Issue permit" | `Watch it say no.` |
| 2–5s | Split: welding sparks / tank entry below | `Hot works. Confined space. Same place.` |

| 16–18s | — | `MySafeOps.` |

**Notes:** the block message must be legible. Zoom in on it. This is the most argument-provoking clip available — expect comments, reply to all of them.

## Tier 2 — Strong follow-ups

### R4 — Eighteen forms, not one
**Length 15s · Screen recording · Persona: surveyor**

Rapid cycle through capture types — trial pit, borehole cap, piezometer, control mark — showing the form changing each time. End on the control mark asking one question.

Overlays: `One form for everything?` → `Trial pit.` → `Borehole cap.` → `Piezometer.` → `Control mark. One question.`

**Why it works:** the visual rhythm of forms changing does the explaining. Fast cuts, no narration.

---

### R5 — Which way were you facing?
**Length 18s · Screen recording · Persona: surveyor**

Open a context-free chamber photo. Overlay: `Which chamber is this?` Then reveal it on the map with the bearing arrow and coverage area shading.

Overlays: `Which chamber is this?` → `Which way were you facing?` → bearing arrow appears → `Recorded. Plotted.`

### R6 — Paper vs tablet
**Length 15s · Before/after · Persona: contractor owner**

Battered RAMS folder pulled from a van footwell, pages stuck together. Cut to the same document on a tablet, signed, current.

Overlays: `"Can I see your RAMS?"` → `Option one.` (folder) → `Option two.` (tablet, ten seconds) → `That's how you get invited back.`

**Visual asset:** existing Drive bank has van and tablet photography — `Post 17 – Client audit` and the RAMS shots.

---

### R7 — The handoff chain
**Length 25s · Animated diagram · Persona: survey owner, contractor**

Five boxes appear one at a time: Survey → Report → PDF → Office re-types → Permit in a van. Each arrow drops a fragment. Then the chain collapses into a single record.

Overlays: `The cable was on the survey.` → `Five handoffs.` → `Two re-types.` → `It still got hit.` → `One record instead.`

**Notes:** the longest concept here and the most important. Worth animating properly. Keep it plain — hand-drawn beats corporate motion graphics for this audience.

---

### R8 — The coarse fix warning
**Length 15s · Screen recording · Persona: surveyor · Build in public**

Phone reporting a poor GPS fix. App throws the warning instead of silently saving.

Overlays: `Your phone said it knew where it was.` → `It was 40 metres out.` → `Now it tells you.` → `Warning, not silence.`

**Why it works:** admitting a limitation reads as competence. Strong trust-builder.

## Tier 3 — Rotation

### R9 — What's in a handover pack
**Length 20s · Screen recording**

### R10 — Permit types you've never seen in software
**Length 18s · Screen recording**

Overlay: `Rail.` → `Marine.` → `Aerial.` → `Fields. Not notes.`

**Length 12s · Screen recording**
Delete a permit. Show it in the recycle bin. Restore it. Show the audit log recording all three actions.
Overlay: `Deleted.` → `Not gone.` → `And it logged all of that.`

**Length 20s · Radargram footage + graphics**
Radargram on screen, then a plastic pipe illustration that doesn't return a signal, then a trench reading as one anomaly.
Overlays: `GPR doesn't see everything.` → `Plastic, no tracer wire.` → `Sometimes it's the trench, not the service.` → `Put it in the quote.`

## Production standards

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical. Shoot everything with 5–6 seconds of headroom and trim in edit.

**Screen recordings:** record at device resolution, don't upscale. Hide any real customer data — use a demo org. Slow the cursor down; natural mouse speed is too fast to follow on a phone.

**Site footage:** handheld, available light, documentary register. It should look like it was shot on site because it was. The existing photo bank on Drive was generated in this style — match it, don't drift into polished commercial lighting.

**Captions:** high contrast, safe from the bottom third where the platform puts its own UI. Sentence case, not all caps. One idea per card.

**Music:** flat and rhythmic, or none. Never epic build-ups — this audience finds them ridiculous.

**Length:** 15–22 seconds is the working range. R7 at 25s is the only justified exception.

**Do not:** use stock footage of people in hard hats pointing at tablets. It reads as fake instantly to anyone in the trade and it will cost more credibility than the Reel earns.
