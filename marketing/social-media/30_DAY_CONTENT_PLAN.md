# 30-Day Content Plan

**22 posts in 30 days.** Not daily — daily posting for its own sake burns content and depresses reach. This is 2–3 LinkedIn and 2–3 Instagram per week, with rest days built in deliberately.

**Strategy:** lead with survey (the sharpest differentiator, least contested), fold safety in as the bundle. See `TARGET_AUDIENCE.md` for the reasoning.

**Weighting across the month:** 35% problem/education · 25% build in public · 20% product demonstration · 15% pain point · 5% industry opinion.

## Rest days are deliberate

Days 3, 6, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24, 28 have nothing scheduled.

Use them to reply to comments. Comment velocity in the first two hours drives LinkedIn reach far harder than posting frequency does. One post with forty real comments beats three posts with four.

---

## What to measure

Ignore vanity metrics. Track these four:

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Comments per post | Both | Whether the topic is contentious enough |
| Saves | Instagram | Whether it's genuinely useful (carousels should win here) |
| Profile visits after post | Both | Whether the hook made anyone curious about you |
| Enquiries, and which post preceded them | Inbox | The only metric that pays |

**Review at day 15 and day 30.** If Instagram is producing reach but no profile visits after four weeks, cut it to one post a week and put the time into LinkedIn comments instead.

## Decisions to make before day 1

1. **Survey-led or safety-led?** This plan assumes survey-led. If you'd rather lead safety, swap days 4 and 11 for permit and RAMS topics and push the PAS 128 material to week 4.
2. **One account or two?** Currently one. If survey posts consistently outperform safety posts on the same account, split them in month two.
3. **The handoff diagram.** Day 4 needs it and so do three other pieces. Build it in week 0.
4. **Screen recordings.** R1, R2 and R3 need a demo org with no real customer data in it. Set that up before week 1.

---

## What is deliberately not in this plan

- **AI features.** The routes are hidden pending prebuilt packs. When they ship, that's a strong build-in-public thread — but announcing them now would be announcing something users can't reach.
- **Any case study or customer name.** Nothing publishable exists yet. When a customer agrees, a real case study displaces two of the week 4 posts immediately — it will outperform anything here.
- **Pricing posts.** Too early. Pricing content works once people know what the product is, not before.
- **Anything with a statistic.** No measured data exists. Every efficiency claim in this month's content is framed as a question or an observation.
